About This Course

This unit provides a comprehensive introduction to crop production in temperate agricultural systems, with particular emphasis on Western Australian cropping systems. The lectures integrate agronomic principles, crop physiology, environmental factors, and practical management strategies.

Lecture 01
Introduction to Cropping Systems
Overview of cropping systems, the farming enterprise context, and the Mediterranean climate challenge facing WA agriculture.
📚 Foundation concepts
Lecture 02
Crop Modelling
Crop simulation models as tools for decision-making, yield prediction, and understanding G × E × M interactions.
🔬 Tools & methodology
Lecture 03
Soil Sampling & Fertiliser Recommendation
Soil testing protocols, nutrient budgeting, and evidence-based fertiliser recommendations for crop production.
🌱 Soil & nutrition
Lecture 04-05
Climate and Australian Agriculture
Climate patterns, rainfall variability, and their impact on Australian cropping systems and management strategies.
🌦️ Environment & climate
Lecture 04
Western Australian Climate
Deep dive into WA's Mediterranean climate, seasonal patterns, rainfall zones, and implications for crop production.
☀️ Regional context
Lecture 06
Agronomy of Cereals
Growth stages, yield formation, compensation mechanisms, water-use efficiency, and grain quality management in cereals.
🌾 Crop production
