# An integrated paddock workflow

1. **Review history.** Collect crops, rotations, weeds, herbicides, disease/nematode results, yields, soil tests, rainfall and margins.
2. **Define constraints and opportunity.** Delineate soils or zones; test fertility, pH, salinity, water and pathogens where this will change the decision.
3. **Choose the rotation position.** Select crop and cultivar for disease, weeds, nitrogen, water, soil fit and market—not just today’s price.
4. **Set targets.** Use seasonal scenarios, stored water, soil capability and crop modelling or water-limited calculations to set yield, quality and nitrogen targets.
5. **Build the agronomy package.** Specify sowing, seed rate, nutrition, stubble, weeds, disease and harvest-quality management.
6. **Apply accurately.** Calibrate seeding and spraying; follow labels and resistance stewardship.
7. **Monitor and diagnose.** Compare good and poor zones, inspect roots and use targeted tests while a response is still useful.
8. **Harvest and learn.** Record yield, quality, costs and spatial patterns; update the gross margin, rotation plan and management zones.

The workflow makes the connections explicit: [[crop-rotations]] affects disease, weeds, water and nitrogen; [[gross-margins]] tests the economic outcome; [[precision-agriculture]] can improve the spatial resolution of each step.
