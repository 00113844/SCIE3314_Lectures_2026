# Global food production

Food demand is shaped by population, diets and income; production is bounded by land, water, climate, technology and environmental limits. The lecture material places ending hunger and poverty alongside managing the impact of human activity on the Earth system.

## Crop roles

- Wheat and rice are major human food staples.
- Maize and soybeans are important food, feed and industrial crops.
- Canola and sunflower are major oilseeds.
- Lupins are a small global commodity in which Australia has a prominent role.

## Concentrated supply

Production and exports are concentrated in a limited number of regions. Weather, conflict, logistics and trade disruption in major grain- and oilseed-producing countries can therefore change world supply and price. The course uses Ukraine and Russia to illustrate this exposure, particularly for wheat, barley, maize and sunflower products.

For Australian growers, local production choices are consequently linked to market specifications, export demand and global price risk.
