# Water, climate and crop modelling

## Rainfall deciles

A decile ranks rainfall against the historical distribution for the same place and period. Decile 1 represents the lowest 10% of records, decile 5 is the median, and decile 10 the highest 10%. Deciles help compare plausible seasonal scenarios instead of treating an average as the only planning case.

## Water-limited yield planning

The lecture uses growing-season rainfall (GSR, commonly April–October) and a simple planning equation:

> **Yield potential = (GSR − evaporation allowance) × water-use efficiency**

Illustrative rules use `(GSR − 110 mm) × 20 kg/ha/mm` for wheat, `(GSR − 90) × 20` for barley, `(GSR − 130) × 15` for pulses and `(GSR − 110) × 15` for oilseeds. A target may be set below potential to reflect risk. These are planning heuristics: stored water, rainfall timing, soil depth and constraints can change the outcome.

## Yield Prophet

Yield Prophet is presented as a crop-modelling decision tool for comparing nitrogen options and yield prospects. Inputs include weather records, soil type and water, soil analysis, variety, sowing details, prior crop and stubble. Its value is in comparing scenarios; output quality is limited by input quality and uncertainty in future weather.
