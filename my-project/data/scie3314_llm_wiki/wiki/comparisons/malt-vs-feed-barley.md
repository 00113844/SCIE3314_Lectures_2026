# Malt versus feed barley

Malt can earn a premium, but it requires an accredited variety and acceptable protein, screenings, test weight, germination, purity, colour and skinning. Feed has broader variety eligibility and fewer quality restrictions.

Choose a malting option only after comparing the premium, relative yield, agronomic and disease fit, and the chance of making grade. A reliable feed crop can be the better enterprise when malt-quality risk is high.
