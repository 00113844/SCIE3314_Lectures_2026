# Resistance versus tolerance

**Tolerance** is the current crop's ability to yield under nematode attack. **Resistance** is the crop's ability to reduce nematode multiplication. A tolerant but susceptible variety can look successful while increasing risk for later crops.

Plan rotations with both traits in mind, after identifying the nematode species.
