# Uniform versus site-specific management

Uniform-rate management treats a paddock as one unit. Site-specific crop management uses zones or real-time sensing to vary inputs according to measured differences in potential or constraints.

Site-specific management is most valuable where variation is substantial, coherent, repeatable and understood. A poorly validated variable-rate map is not better than a well-executed uniform practice.
