# Gross Margin Analysis and Profitability in WA Cropping Systems

## Summary
Gross margin analysis is a fundamental economic tool used by Western Australian agronomists to evaluate the profitability of specific cropping enterprises by subtracting total variable costs from gross income. In the context of precision agriculture, this analysis is evolving from a paddock-wide average to zone-specific calculations, allowing farmers to optimize input applications based on site-specific yield potential.

## Key Facts
- **Gross Margin Calculation**: Defined as Gross Income (Yield $\times$ Grain Price) minus Total Variable Costs.
- **Variable Costs**: Includes all direct inputs such as seed, seed treatments, fertilizers, herbicides, machinery operations, freight, and casual labor.
- **Excluded Costs**: Fixed overheads, including permanent labor, machinery depreciation, insurance, and taxes, are excluded from gross margin calculations.
- **Predictive Modeling**: Tools such as Yield Prophet are utilized to model potential yields and determine optimal nitrogen application rates for specific paddocks.
- **Financial Risk Management**: Sensitivity analysis, including the calculation of breakeven prices and breakeven yields, is used to assess the viability of a crop under fluctuating market conditions.
- **Precision Integration**: Precision agriculture enables the analysis of gross margins within specific paddock zones, facilitating variable rate application (VRA) of inputs to maximize efficiency and profitability.
- **Industry Support**: The Grains Research and Development Corporation (GRDC) provides standardized Excel templates and research to assist growers in calculating and improving on-farm profitability.

## Local Examples
- **Crop Benchmarks**: Australian Premium White (APW) wheat with 2021 forecast prices of $350/t and yield benchmarks of 1.8, 3.2, and 4.8 t/ha for low, medium, and high production zones, respectively.
- **Regional Modeling**: Use of the Lake Naman weather station (near Regans Ford) for localized yield and nitrogen modeling via Yield Prophet.
- **Industry Resources**: Use of the Farmanko annual book for updated chemical pricing and recommendations, and consultation with agencies such as Nutrien, Landmark, or Elders.
