# No-Till Seeding Systems and Strategic Management in Western Australia

## Summary
No-till farming in Western Australia is a strategic system designed to maximize water use efficiency by converting soil moisture directly into grain yield. It integrates minimal soil disturbance and residue retention with precision technologies, such as RTK-GPS and variable rate application, to maintain soil structure and productivity in arid Mediterranean climates.

## Key Facts
- **Core Components**: The system relies on five pillars: reduced soil disturbance, surface residue retention, diverse crop rotations, cultural weed control, and controlled traffic.
- **Moisture Conservation**: Retained stubble acts as an insulating blanket that reduces evaporation, dissipates the kinetic energy of raindrops to prevent soil sealing, and minimizes runoff.
- **Seeding Mechanisms**: 
    - *Knife Point (Tine) Systems*: Use tungsten-tipped points to create small grooves; these systems throw herbicide-treated soil away from the row, creating a protected zone for the crop.
    - *Disc Systems*: Utilize leading coulters and double disc openers for minimal disturbance, though they lack the herbicide-free banding provided by tine systems.
- **Strategic Tillage**: While no-till is the baseline, remedial tillage (e.g., mouldboard ploughing, deep ripping, or delving) is used to address non-wetting sandy soils, bury resistant weed seeds, or incorporate lime into acidic subsoils.
- **Soil Constraints**: Tillage in duplex soils may involve "delving" to bring clay from depths exceeding one meter to the surface to improve the water-holding capacity of sandy topsoils.
- **Precision Integration**: Controlled Traffic Farming (CTF) utilizes RTK-GPS guidance (accurate to 2 cm) to confine all machinery traffic to permanent tramlines, eliminating compaction in the crop growing zone.

## Local Examples
- **Colin Hutchinson (Urukoin)**: Demonstrated the efficacy of strict no-till and CTF, achieving a water use efficiency of 17 kg/mm/ha (compared to a regional average of 9 kg/mm/ha).
- **Rod Messina (Minunu)**: Exemplified the use of strategic mouldboard ploughing to remediate non-wetting soils and manage herbicide-resistant weed banks.
- **Non-Wetting Soil Observations**: Field demonstrations at Una showed water pooling for over 2.5 hours on non-tilled sandy soils, highlighting the need for strategic intervention in specific soil types.
