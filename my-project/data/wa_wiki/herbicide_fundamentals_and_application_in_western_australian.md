# Herbicide Fundamentals and Application in Western Australian Cropping Systems

## Summary
Herbicides are essential components of Western Australian broadacre cropping, serving as the primary technological enabler for no-tillage farming systems. By replacing mechanical cultivation, herbicides allow for the preservation of soil structure, reduction of wind erosion, and significant improvements in water use efficiency. Precision management of these chemicals requires a deep understanding of herbicide chemistry, environmental dissipation, and the physiological interactions between the chemical and the target weed.

## Key Facts
- **Role in No-Till Systems**: Herbicides substitute destructive soil cultivation, allowing for early seeding into stubble and the maintenance of soil moisture and structure.
- **Application Categories**: Herbicides are classified as non-selective (broad-spectrum) or selective, and further divided by timing into pre-emergence (soil-applied/residual) and post-emergence (foliar uptake).
- **Environmental Dissipation**: Chemical efficacy is reduced through photodecomposition (UV radiation), volatilization (driven by vapor pressure), surface runoff, and soil adsorption.
- **Soil Interaction**: Adsorption is heavily influenced by soil pH, iron oxides, and organic matter; high organic matter soils may require higher application rates due to stronger chemical binding.
- **Moisture Dependency**: Soil-applied herbicides require moisture for activation; prolonged dry periods following application can lead to chemical breakdown before weed germination occurs.
- **Positional Safety**: In no-till systems, certain herbicides (e.g., trifluralin) rely on positional safety, remaining in the interrow spaces during seeding to avoid direct contact with the crop.
- **Foliar Uptake Factors**: Post-emergence efficacy is highest on young, unstressed plants and is influenced by leaf morphology (waxiness, hairs) and the use of surfactants to reduce droplet reflection.

## Local Examples
- **Liberty Link Canola**: A genetically modified variety utilized in WA for tolerance to glufosinate.
- **Roundup Ready Crops**: GM varieties allowing for broad-spectrum weed control via glyphosate without crop injury.
