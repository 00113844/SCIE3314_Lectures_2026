# Soil Acidity Management and Decision Support in Western Australia

## Summary
Soil acidity is a primary environmental constraint in Western Australian cropping systems, leading to nutrient deficiencies and aluminum toxicity that significantly reduce yield potential. Precision management is facilitated by decision-support tools such as the iLime app, which enables growers to model long-term pH trajectories and optimize the economic return on investment for lime applications.

## Key Facts
- **Critical pH Thresholds**: Surface soils below pH 5.5 and subsoils below pH 4.8 are critical thresholds; the latter often triggers the release of toxic aluminum, which severely inhibits root elongation and moisture uptake.
- **Economic Impact**: Soil acidification is estimated to cost the WA agricultural industry between $500 and $1,500 million annually in lost yield income.
- **The iLime Tool**: Developed from the "Time to Lime" project, this app allows for the simulation of pH changes and profitability over 20-year periods, demonstrating high predictive accuracy (R-squared of 0.8) against long-term trial data.
- **Liming Trade-offs**: While liming improves nutrient availability, it can induce manganese deficiency (requiring foliar correction) and may increase the prevalence of snails and take-all disease.
- **Acidification Drivers**: Soil acidity is accelerated by the export of basic cations in harvested grains and the leaching of nitrates, which releases protons into the soil profile.
- **Deep Soil Re-engineering**: Experimental interventions involving the incorporation of lime and organic matter up to 90 cm are being trialed to address combined acidity and compaction constraints.

## Local Examples
- **Avon River Basin**: A large-scale project involving 139 farms and 22,500 soil samples to analyze the relationship between soil pH, nutrient availability, and the reduction of eutrophication in waterways.
- **Eastern Wheatbelt**: A region where high soil variability necessitates zoned management and rigorous ground-truthing of digital lime recommendations.
