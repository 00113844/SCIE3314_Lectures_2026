# Agronomic Planning, Yield Potential, and Soil Constraints in Western Australia

## Summary
This topic covers the integration of water-limited yield calculations, soil physical and chemical constraints, and herbicide resistance management to optimize crop selection in Western Australian dryland systems. It emphasizes the use of precision diagnostics, such as soil DNA testing and bulk density measurements, to align input levels with the actual yield potential of the land.

## Key Facts
- **Water-Limited Yield Potential**: In WA, wheat yield potential is estimated at approximately 20 kg of grain per mm of available water. Available water is calculated as the Growing Season Rainfall (GSR, April to October) minus estimated evaporation (e.g., 110 mm for wheat, 90 mm for barley, and 130 mm for pulses).
- **Target Yielding**: Farmers typically target 75% of the maximum yield potential to account for local environmental constraints and historical rainfall variability.
- **Herbicide Resistance Management**: Resistance is primarily driven by the repeated use of specific modes of action. Group 1 (ACCase inhibitors) and Group 2 (ALS inhibitors) are identified as high-risk groups, necessitating strict rotation to maintain efficacy.
- **Soil Chemical Constraints**: Low soil pH increases the availability of toxic aluminum, which severely limits root growth. Narrow-leaf lupins are specifically utilized for their tolerance to acidic sandy soils, whereas chickpeas require more alkaline, heavier soils.
- **Soil Physical Properties**: Water holding capacity is determined by soil texture, with heavy clays holding significantly more volumetric water (~40%) than sands (~7%). Bulk density measurements are required to convert gravimetric water content to volumetric water content.
- **Pathogen Diagnostics**: Soil DNA testing (e.g., Predicta B) allows for the proactive quantification of risks associated with Rhizoctonia, take-all, and cereal cyst nematode (CCN) before sowing.

## Local Examples
- **Cundadine**: Cited as a location with a Decile 5 rainfall of 207 mm, used as a benchmark for target yield calculations.
- **Predicta B**: A SARDI-developed soil DNA testing service used in WA to assess pathogen and nematode risk levels.
- **Yield Prophet**: A modeling tool used by WA growers to set target yields and determine input levels.
- **CSBP**: A primary provider of soil testing and nutrient data for WA farmers.
