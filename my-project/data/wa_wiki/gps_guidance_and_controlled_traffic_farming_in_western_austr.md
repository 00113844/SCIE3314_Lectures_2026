# GPS Guidance and Controlled Traffic Farming in Western Australia

## Summary
This topic examines the integration of high-accuracy satellite guidance systems and Controlled Traffic Farming (CTF) to optimize field efficiency and soil health in Western Australian broadacre systems. By utilizing Real-Time Kinematic (RTK) GPS, producers can minimize soil compaction and improve input precision, directly enhancing yield potential and water infiltration.

## Key Facts
- **RTK GPS Accuracy:** Real-Time Kinematic (RTK) systems provide a precision of approximately 2 cm, which is critical for precise row alignment and the maintenance of permanent tramlines.
- **Soil Compaction Mitigation:** Uncontrolled traffic can compact up to 50% of a paddock's area; CTF reduces this wheeled area to approximately 15%, significantly improving root exploration and water infiltration.
- **Equipment Synchronization:** Successful CTF requires machinery widths to be matched or be multiples of one another (e.g., a 9m seeder paired with a 27m sprayer) to ensure all wheels remain on designated tracks.
- **Shielded Spraying:** GPS-enabled shielded spraying allows for the application of broad-spectrum herbicides (e.g., paraquat or glyphosate) within crop rows, providing a tool for managing herbicide resistance.
- **Seeding Placement Strategies:** High-accuracy guidance allows farmers to choose between inter-row seeding for better stubble management or seeding on/near previous rows to target residual soil moisture during dry starts.
- **Yield Impact:** Research indicates that soil compaction in wheeled areas causes statistically significant yield reductions that are often not fully restored even after mechanical ripping.

## Local Examples
- **Regional Research:** Studies conducted by Paul Blackwell (Department of Agriculture) identified significant yield differences between wheeled and unwheeled areas in the Esperance and Geraldton regions.
- **Crop-Specific Application:** The use of shielded spraying to protect lupin crops while managing weeds with broad-spectrum herbicides.
