# Plant Tissue Analysis and Nutrient Interpretation in Western Australia

## Summary
Plant tissue analysis serves as a critical diagnostic tool for assessing the real-time nutrient status of crops within Western Australian agricultural systems. By sampling specific leaves at precise phenological stages, agronomists can identify nutrient deficiencies or toxicities, allowing for timely corrective interventions to minimize yield loss.

## Key Facts
- **Standard Reference:** The "Plant Analysis and Interpretation Manual" by Reuter and Robinson is the primary benchmark for nutrient levels and toxic concentrations across Australian crops.
- **Cereal Sampling Protocol:** For wheat and barley, sampling occurs between mid-tillering and stem elongation, targeting the youngest fully expanded leaf (youngest mature blade) to accurately assess immobile trace elements.
- **Canola Sampling Protocol:** Sampling is conducted during the rosette stage, focusing on the youngest fully developed leaf from the middle or upper canopy.
- **Cereal Nutrient Thresholds:** 
    - **Nitrogen (N):** Adequate levels range from 3.5–6.9%; below 3.4% is considered deficient.
    - **Phosphorus (P):** Adequate levels are 0.3–0.5%; below 0.24% is deficient.
    - **Potassium (K):** Levels below 1.5% indicate deficiency.
    - **Sulfur (S):** Levels at or below 0.15% are considered marginal.
- **Canola Nutrient Indicators:** Nitrate levels below 1500 mg/kg are classified as marginal, signaling potential nitrogen deficiency.
- **Micronutrient Measurement:** Due to low concentrations, micronutrients (e.g., Mo, Cu, Zn, B) are measured in milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg) or parts per million (ppm).
- **Sodium (Na) Dynamics:** While sodium is not an essential nutrient, excessive levels can interfere with the uptake of potassium, potentially inducing a K deficiency.

## Local Examples
- **Reuter and Robinson Manual:** The industry-standard guide used by WA agronomists for interpreting tissue data.
- **BASF Growth Stage Guides:** Utilized as a practical tool for timing canola sampling during the rosette stage.
- **Saline-Prone Areas:** In specific WA districts, monitoring sodium levels is critical due to the risk of sodium toxicity affecting potassium uptake.
