# Land Preparation and Sowing Management in Western Australia

## Summary
Effective land preparation in Western Australia integrates precise nutrient application, strategic stubble retention, and optimized sowing windows to maximize yield potential. This process requires a technical understanding of seed physiology, cultivar selection, and the environmental constraints of diverse rainfall zones to ensure successful crop establishment.

## Key Facts
- **Fertilizer Composition**: Solid fertilizers are categorized as compounds (uniform nutrient distribution per granule) or blends (single-nutrient granules, more cost-effective but prone to segregation). Liquid fertilizers, such as Flexi-N, provide high-density nitrogen application.
- **Stubble Management**: To minimize erosion and maintain soil health, stubble retention of 1.5 to 2 tonnes per hectare (approximately 75% soil cover) is required. Burning stubble is discouraged as it results in a 90% loss of nitrogen.
- **Sowing Timing and Yield**: Sowing dates are critical for wheat; delays beyond mid-May can result in yield losses of 5–10% per week. Optimal flowering for wheat occurs between early September and early October to avoid frost and terminal heat/water stress.
- **Plant Density Targets**: Target densities vary by crop and environment:
    - **Wheat**: 150–200 plants/m² in high-rainfall areas; ~100 plants/m² in low-rainfall areas.
    - **Canola**: 20–35 plants/m² (lower for hybrids).
    - **Lentils/Chickpeas**: 35–45 plants/m².
- **Sowing Depth and Spacing**: Sowing depth is governed by seed size and coleoptile length. Row spacing typically ranges from 22 cm (coastal/high-rainfall) to 30 cm (inland/dryland) to balance yield potential with risk stability.
- **Post-Seeding Monitoring**: Agronomic checks are conducted at 3–4 weeks (emergence and weed pressure) and 6–10 weeks (nutrition and vigor) to determine if dynamic nitrogen adjustments are required.

## Local Examples
- **Flexi-N**: A liquid nitrogen fertilizer containing 32% N (w/w) with a bulk density of 1.32 t/m³.
- **Wheat Cultivars**: Seed weight variation is evident between varieties such as Justica Plus (34 mg/seed) and Calingari (40 mg/seed).
- **DAP**: Diammonium phosphate is utilized not only for phosphorus but also as a source of sulfur and zinc.
