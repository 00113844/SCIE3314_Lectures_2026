# Soil Nutrient Analysis and Management in Western Australia

## Summary
This topic covers the technical principles of soil nutrient profiling and the chemical constraints affecting nutrient availability in Western Australian dryland systems. It emphasizes the transition from traditional soil testing to precision diagnostic tools to optimize fertilizer application and soil pH correction.

## Key Facts
- **Nutrient Mobility:** Nutrients exhibit varying degrees of mobility in the soil profile; Nitrogen (N) is highly mobile and prone to leaching, Potassium (K) is intermediate, and Phosphorus (P) is relatively immobile, often concentrating in the upper soil layers.
- **pH and Nutrient Availability:** The optimal pH range for most crops is 5.5–7.0. Phosphorus availability is severely restricted in acidic soils (below pH 5.5) due to binding with aluminum and iron, and in highly alkaline soils (above pH 8.5).
- **Phosphorus Buffering Index (PBI):** PBI measures a soil's capacity to "lock up" phosphorus. High PBI soils require higher critical phosphorus levels (38–40 mg/kg) to maintain adequate crop nutrition compared to low PBI soils (20–30 mg/kg).
- **Diagnostic Testing:** While the Colwell test is the standard for P and K in WA, Diffusion Gradient in Thin Films (DGT) is emerging as a more reliable measure of P availability across a broader pH range.
- **Potassium Depletion:** Many WA soils are experiencing potassium depletion, with levels below 50 mg/kg identified as deficient for cereal crops.
- **Sowing Dynamics:** Target plant densities vary by crop: wheat (120–200 plants/m²), lupins (35–40 plants/m²), and canola (10–35 plants/m²). Sowing timing is used strategically to avoid pathogens, such as delaying field peas and chickpeas to avoid black spot spore showers.

## Local Examples
- **Flexi-N:** A commonly used liquid Urea Ammonium Nitrate (UAN) fertilizer in WA with a nitrogen content of 42% by weight/volume.
- **iLime App:** A digital tool used by WA growers to determine lime application rates based on soil pH and depth.
- **Colwell Test:** The standard soil test used in Western Australia for measuring available phosphorus and potassium.

Wheat, Canola, Lupins, Field Peas, Chickpeas
