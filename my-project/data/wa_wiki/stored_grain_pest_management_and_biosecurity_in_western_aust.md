# Stored Grain Pest Management and Biosecurity in Western Australia

## Summary
Stored grain management in Western Australia is a critical component of agricultural biosecurity, ensuring that the state's high export volume (approximately 90% of total grain production) meets international "nil tolerance" standards for live insects. The system relies on a combination of sealed storage infrastructure and chemical fumigation, with a growing emphasis on monitoring phosphine resistance and transitioning from calendar-based to ecologically timed treatments.

## Key Facts
- **Export Requirements:** WA adheres to a federally mandated nil tolerance for live insects in export grain to maintain market access and premium quality status.
- **Infrastructure:** The transition from bag storage to sealed bulk storage systems allows for effective fumigation and prevents the ingress of pests. Modern on-farm storages often incorporate thermosiphons to facilitate air circulation and uniform fumigant distribution.
- **Primary Fumigant:** Phosphine is the dominant chemical control agent due to its high penetration capability in dense grain masses and low residue profile.
- **Pest Profile:** The lesser grain borer and rust red flour beetle are the primary pests in WA; the former is particularly damaging due to its ability to bore through the grain.
- **Resistance Dynamics:** Phosphine resistance is increasing by approximately 5% annually, driven by high selection pressure and the introduction of resistant strains via imported commodities.
- **Ecological Risks:** Research indicates that grain pests can survive in native bushland far from storages, presenting a constant risk of reinfestation.

## Local Examples
- **CBH Group:** Utilizes large-scale storage facilities (some holding up to 285,000 tonnes) and historically implemented calendar-based fumigation every three months.
- **Agriculture Protection Board:** Provided the legislative framework that allowed WA to implement stricter pest management controls on farms compared to eastern Australian states.
- **Diagnostic Tools:** Development of a WA-specific, rapid same-day phosphine resistance test to provide immediate feedback to growers.
