# Crop rotation logic and diversification

Why crop sequence is one of the most powerful and cheapest management levers, the classic WA
rotations, and why systems remain wheat-dominated despite the agronomic case for diversity.

## Key points taught (L01)
- Disease break: host-specific pathogens lose their host; herbicide MOA rotation and resistance
  management; nitrogen credit from grain legumes
- Rotational value shows up in the following crop, not the break crop's own gross margin
- Classic rotations: cereal-legume, cereal-canola, longer diversified sequences, pasture phase on
  mixed farms
- Wheat's low-risk, liquid-market advantage vs break-crop (pulse) risk
- Herbicide resistance in ryegrass as an accelerating diversification driver
