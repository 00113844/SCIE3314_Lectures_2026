# Water-use efficiency and the yield gap

Water as the first limiting resource in dryland WA cropping, the French-Schultz WUE benchmark, and
the yield gap it exposes between water-limited potential and actual yield.

## Key points taught (L01)
- Water as the yield-ceiling resource in dryland systems; crop water balance (transpiration vs
  evaporation, runoff, drainage)
- WUE definition: kg grain per hectare per mm
- French and Schultz (1984) framework; ~110mm soil evaporation constant; transpiration efficiency
  slope (20 to 22-25 kg/ha/mm)
- Yield gap diagnosis: genetic vs agronomic routes to closing it
- Yield as a probability distribution, not a single number; exceedance curves; seasonal-outlook-
  based investment — sets up the risk-management theme
