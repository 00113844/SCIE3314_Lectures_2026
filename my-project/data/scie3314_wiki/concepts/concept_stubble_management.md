# Stubble management trade-offs

Retain, reduce, or burn — the L01 closing case study in systems trade-offs, since the decision
touches erosion, moisture, disease, nitrogen and herbicide efficacy simultaneously.

## Key points taught (L01)
- Retention benefits: erosion control, moisture, organic matter
- Reasons to reduce or burn: nitrogen tie-up, disease carryover (blackleg, crown rot, yellow
  spot), herbicide interception
- Burning as a targeted disease/weed-seed destruction tool
- Framed explicitly as the unit's closing trade-off message: every decision balances competing
  biological, operational and economic objectives
