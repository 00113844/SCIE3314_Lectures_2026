# WA grainbelt and the Mediterranean seasonal rhythm

The geographic, economic and climatic setting for the whole unit: WA's grainbelt scale and export
orientation (LO1), and the Mediterranean climate pattern that governs management timing (LO2).

## Key points taught (L01)
- WA grainbelt geography (Geraldton to Esperance, ~18M ha)
- Export orientation (80-90% of 15-20 Mt grain)
- Large, mechanised, low-labour farms; poor ancient soils, winter rainfall, no irrigation
- Mediterranean climate: cool wet winters, hot dry summers
- The autumn break and growing-season rainfall; terminal drought and spring grain fill
- Operation timing windows across the season
