# SCIE3314 Unit Learning Outcomes

Canonical outcome set for mapping lecture and subtopic `key_concepts` against. Sourced from the
Bloom's-tagged LO1-LO6 descriptions in the official unit outline (confirmed by Gustavo as the
primary set, "mainly - can be extended" — treat as the backbone, not a hard ceiling; lecture-level
outcomes can add finer-grained targets underneath these six).

| ID | Cognitive level (Bloom's) | Outcome |
|----|---------------------------|---------|
| LO1 | Comprehension | Explain the role of Australian and Western Australian grain production within global food supply systems, and identify the environmental, economic, and social factors that shape the structure and management of broadacre cropping enterprises. |
| LO2 | Analysis | Analyse how Mediterranean-type climate patterns, soil constraints, and seasonal water balance dynamics in WA interact to determine crop management timing, variety selection, and production risk. |
| LO3 | Application | Apply principles of conservation agriculture, soil fertility management, cereal phenology, and break-crop agronomy to develop management recommendations appropriate to the conditions and constraints of WA broadacre farming systems. |
| LO4 | Evaluation | Assess the biological and economic impact of weeds, fungal diseases, insect pests, and soil-borne pathogens on grain production in WA, and formulate integrated management strategies that account for resistance mechanisms, economic thresholds, and environmental conditions. |
| LO5 | Application + Analysis | Demonstrate competence in using a process-based crop simulation model (APSIM) to predict crop growth, water use, and nitrogen response under varying management and climate scenarios, and critically evaluate model outputs to inform agronomic decision-making. |
| LO6 | Synthesis | Synthesise agronomic, environmental, financial, and crop protection knowledge to construct a professional agronomic management package for a major WA grain crop, including a gross margin analysis and a quantitative nitrogen management strategy. |

## Where each outcome is actually taught

Not every outcome is primarily a lecture-content outcome — several live mainly in labs, the
glasshouse trial, or the farm visit. The concept wiki should not force a lecture concept to cover
an outcome that's really delivered elsewhere.

| ID | Primary delivery mode | Notes |
|----|------------------------|-------|
| LO1 | Lecture | Global/WA context — L01, L04, L14 |
| LO2 | Lecture | Climate, water balance — L01, L05 |
| LO3 | Lecture + glasshouse trial (Cereal Derby) | Agronomy content across most lectures; applied in the pot trial |
| LO4 | Lecture | Weeds, disease, pests — L09, L12, and the guest-delivered disease/pest content |
| LO5 | Computer lab (APSIM) | Six structured Thursday lab sessions; L02 (Crop Modelling) is the lecture anchor but the skill itself is lab-taught |
| LO6 | Farm visit + Agronomic Package assessment | Synthesis assessment; L10 (Financial/Risk) and L13 (Process/Logistics) are the closest lecture anchors |

## Compact (CAIDI/assessment-record) outcome list

The official assessment-weighting record uses a shorter numbered list tied directly to each
assessment item. It describes the same six outcomes at lower resolution — kept here for
cross-reference since assessment mapping (below) uses these numbers, not the LO1-6 IDs.

| # | Outcome (compact) | Assessed by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Explain the place of Australian agriculture in world food production | Online quizzes |
| 2 | Recall key characteristics and agronomy of major crops grown in WA | Agronomy report |
| 3 | Articulate how agricultural systems can be managed for more sustainable production | Agronomy report, online quizzes |
| 4 | Explain crop and weed models and how they simulate complex agricultural systems | Agronomy report, modelling assignment |
| 5 | Explain the impact of major pathogens/weeds on WA crops and formulate control | Agronomy report, modelling + field-day assignments, online quizzes |
| 6 | Demonstrate critical thinking at a farming systems scale | Agronomy report, modelling + field-day assignments, online quizzes |

Assessment-to-outcome mapping (compact numbering): Farm Interview → 1-2, 6. Agronomic Package →
all. Cereal Derby → 3, 5-6. Weed Smart Modules → 2, 4. Weed Modeling (RIM & WSW) → 4, 5. APSIM
Modeling → 5, 6.

## Relationship to `data/Learning_Outcomes.md`

`data/Learning_Outcomes.md` is an older, unstructured working document (flat bullet list plus
loose per-topic groupings, no IDs). It's more granular in places (e.g. specific French-Schultz,
blackleg, and herbicide-resistance points) but predates this canonical LO1-6 set and shouldn't be
treated as authoritative. Where its detail is useful for a specific lecture's `key_concepts`, pull
it in at the subtopic level rather than citing it as a top-level outcome.
